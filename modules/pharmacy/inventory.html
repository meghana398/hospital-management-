How the flags work

  • Running low: total stock across batches is at or below the reorder level. Daily use is the average of the last 30 days of dispensing and issues.
  • Expiring soon: the batch expires within 90 days. It is given out first when you dispense.
  • Expired: blocked from sale in Dispense and bill. Return it to the supplier from Purchase requests.
